3 · Entry timing — when to write / sell

Two different jobs, so two ladders. CSP is an entry — catch a bounce off a bottom to get assigned cheap. CC is income — write calls on shares you already hold, so it should fire whenever the premium is worth it and the stock won't run away, not only at rare tops. Both score 0–12 and drive the light in §5 (a low timing score never reds a leg on its own — see §5).

4 · LEAP — a buy, not premium income

A LEAP is accumulation in a confirmed uptrend — often on a pullback. It qualifies on just two hard checks (an uptrend and a workable contract); everything else is a graded 0–11 score, never a hard reject. A LEAP is never shown red.

ElementRule
Delta0.75–0.80 (deep ITM = intrinsic-led)
DTE180–900 · target ~542 (Jan-2027+)
Gate 1 — Uptrendprice > 200-MA and 50-MA > 200-MA. Below the 50-MA is fine — a pullback inside the uptrend (§2).
Gate 2 — Contract backstoptime value (premium − intrinsic) ≤ 55% of premium — reject a mostly-rented-time call

Score (0–11), all transparent: IV vs realized (<1.0 → 3 · <1.25 → 2) · RSI §2 zone (45–55 → 3 · 33–45 → 2 · 55–65 → 1) · time value (≤25% → 2 · ≤40% → 1) · carry = (time value ÷ spot)×365/DTE (≤5% → 2 · ≤8% → 1) · uptrend confirmed → 1. Carry and time value cost points but no longer gate — only the 55% backstop can reject a contract.

5 · The traffic light — how it all rolls up

Every suggestion carries one colour — a scorecard rollup of the rules above, not a single gate. It's information for your own decision, not a command: amber means analyze.

LightCSP / CCLEAP
STRONG tradeable and a full setup — right side of median, in-fit, score ≥ 8 (§3) qualifies (§4) and entry score ≥ 8/11
ANALYZE tradeable, but the setup is partial — wrong side of median, or timing < 8 — your call qualifies, entry score 5–7/11 — your call
BLOCKED a hard NO only — earnings inside the trade, thin premium, or no liquidity — (a LEAP is never red)
OFF-TREND — not in an uptrend, or no workable contract — grey, not a buy zone (§4)

Red is reserved for a hard NO — a reason not to place the trade at all: earnings within the trade, thin premium (below the §1 minimum), or no liquidity (OI or bid/ask). Everything else — the median side, delta/DTE fit, a weak timing score — caps at ANALYZE (amber), never red. A LEAP is never red either: it qualifies (green/amber by its score /11, shown beside the dot) or reads OFF-TREND (grey). Every node in a tile's hover is exactly what the colour rolls up.
