3 · Entry timing — the mean-reversion setup score

Entry timing = is now a good moment to sell? A turnaround off an extreme (in your favour) with premium richer than the stock's actual moves — not just the right side of the median. Points accrue; 🟢 FULL ≥10 · 🟡 PARTIAL ≥6 · 🟠 building ≥3 · 🔴 none. Only ≥6 is listed as a suggestion.

4 · LEAP — a buy, not premium income

Cost is judged in extrinsic value (the dollars you pay for time), not IV — IV is only the input that inflates it. A LEAP qualifies only if it passes A and C and the trend.

ElementRule
Delta0.75–0.80 (deep ITM = intrinsic-led)
DTE180–900 · target ~542 (Jan-2027+)
Gate A — Time value ≤ 40%extrinsic (premium − intrinsic) ≤ 40% of premium
Gate C — Carry ≤ 8%/yrcarry = (extrinsic ÷ spot) × 365/DTE — the time-value you pay per year, as % of the share price
Clear uptrendprice > 50-MA · price > 200-MA · 50-MA > 200-MA

Entry timing (the mean-reversion setup) is informational only on a LEAP — it is not a gate. A LEAP qualifies on Gate A + Gate C + a clear uptrend; RSI-rising is shown as extra colour, not a pass/fail.
